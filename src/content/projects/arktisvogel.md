**Adipöser Arktisvogel** is a party game for Android and iOS that I designed and built with Flutter.

One player reads a question out loud and has to guess how the others will answer: yes or no, this or that. Guess right and the others drink, guess wrong and you do. The real point is the conversations that follow.

- Over 400 questions in German and English, in seven categories, from harmless to spicy
- Questions are updated online, no app update needed
- Players can suggest their own questions directly in the app
- Free with ads, or ad-free with the Pro version

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over.

Coming soon to Google Play and the App Store.
